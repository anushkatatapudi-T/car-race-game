🏁 HOW TO RACE

Follow the neon track and pass through checkpoints. Complete all laps to finish the race. Avoid going off-track — it slows you down!

⚡ NITRO & EMP

Press SPACE to activate your nitro boost. Press E to trigger EMP Shockwave that slows down nearby AI cars!

🟡 COLLECTIBLES

Collect coins for currency. Grab nitro pickups to refuel. Use shield pickups for temporary protection from collisions.

🏆 SCORING

Earn points from your finishing position, coins collected, drift scores, and clean driving. Use coins in the Garage to buy new cars and upgrades!
