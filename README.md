NEON RUSH 🏎️
Race. Drift. Boost. Survive.
NEON RUSH is a futuristic neon-themed arcade racing game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Choose your car and track, race against AI opponents, collect coins, activate nitro boosts, and progress through career mode.
✨ Features
- Quick Race — Choose a car, track, and difficulty before racing.
- Career Mode — Progress through races and work toward completing your career.
- Time Trial — Race against the clock and improve your best time.
- Garage — Unlock cars and purchase performance upgrades using collected coins.
- Multiple Visual Themes — Choose Cyberpunk, Synthwave, Matrix, or Overdrive.
- Nitro Boost — Use a burst of speed during a race.
- EMP Pulse — Slow nearby AI cars with an EMP shockwave.
- Collectibles — Collect coins, nitro pickups, and temporary shields.
- Race HUD — View position, lap, timer, coins, speed, nitro level, drift score, and minimap.
- Settings — Configure sound effects, music, minimap, particle effects, and the directional control HUD.
- Race Results — Review finishing position, time, coins, score, and XP.
- Keyboard Controls — Drive, steer, boost, and navigate menus using the keyboard.
🕹️ Controls
Key	Action
W or ↑	Accelerate / forward
S or ↓	Brake / move backward
A or ←	Steer left
D or →	Steer right
SPACE	Nitro boost
E	EMP pulse blast
ENTER	Select / start
ESC	Pause or go back


🚦 Game Modes
Quick Race
Select an available car and track, choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty, and start a race.
Career
Take on career races and work toward unlocking further progress.
Time Trial
Choose a track and try to complete it in your best time.
Garage
Browse cars, use earned coins to unlock available vehicles, and improve car performance with upgrades.

▶️ How to Run
1. Download or clone the project files.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, and game.js in the correct relative locations.
3. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
For a development workflow, you can also open the project folder in Visual Studio Code and use a local development server, such as the Live Server extension.

An internet connection may be needed to load the Google Fonts used by the interface.

💡 Gameplay Tips
- Follow the track and pass through checkpoints.
- Complete all laps to finish the race.
- Avoid leaving the track, as this can slow you down.
- Use nitro strategically to gain speed.
- Activate EMP near AI opponents to slow them down.
- Collect coins and pickups while racing.
- Spend earned coins in the Garage to unlock cars and purchase upgrades.
⚙️ Settings

- Theme palette

- Minimap visibility
